# F9: Backtest engine + CostModel

Status: done (all slices merged; T2 SIGN-OFF with notes 2026-11-24, PR 158, test-only). One gap stays open: the as-of lot/tick lookup (AC 5, TS-F9-2) is not built. Plan: `.agents/plans/F9-backtest-costmodel.md`. Policy: `docs/strategy/F9-backtest-policy.md`. Not investment advice: a backtest is evidence, not a promise.

Summary: run a strategy over fixture candles net of real charges and slippage, with a rolling walk-forward and a trial count. Owner area: `crates/backtest`, `config/backtest.toml`, `fixtures/{charges,backtest}`.

## Decisions
- GST base = brokerage + exchange txn + SEBI (settles QUAN-65-3/66-1); DP once per scrip per day on the first delivery sell, GST-inclusive (QUAN-66-2); STT and stamp whole rupee half-up; SEBI tested with 0.05 tolerance (rule UNVERIFIED); charges by trade date, no fallback unless `history_policy = latest_for_all` (flagged `costs_approximate`).
- Policy decisions (strategist, 2026-11-20), all in `config/backtest.toml` with `source` and `unverified = true`, never in code:
  - Q1 costs before 2026-11-20: confirm; `latest_for_all` is a per-run opt-in, such runs are "exploratory" and can never reach the evidence bar.
  - Q2 slippage: per class (equity max(0.05%, 1 tick) entry, max(0.10%, 1 tick) stop; index futures 1 / 2 ticks; index options max(2 ticks, 0.5%) / max(3 ticks, 1%)); an unknown class is a typed error; the evidence bar is re-run at 2x slippage and must keep expectancy above zero.
  - Q3 walk-forward: rolling, 24 months in-sample, 6 months out-of-sample, step 6 months, at least 4 folds, warm-up (250 bars) excluded; the trade minimum is pooled: at least 30 OOS trades across folds and instruments (equals risk.toml `min_oos_trades`, a hard floor); a fold under 5 trades is "thin" and left out of the profitable-folds vote. The plan's "30 per fold" is dropped.
  - Q4 lot/tick history: latest snapshot at or before the trade date; none = typed error. **Policy only, not built** (see Known limits, TS-F9-2).
  - Fills: stop long = `min(stop, open)`, short = `max(stop, open)`, then stop slippage (QUAN-77-8); stop wins a tie; a target fills at the target only if the bar trades through it by 1 tick.
  - Trials: a trial is a distinct (strategy id, full parameter set) evaluated in-sample; `prior_trials` is owner-declared; penalty on the profit factor bar: 2-10 trials +0.1, 11-50 +0.2 and label capped at Provisional, over 50 +0.3 and capped. Never loosens.
  - Evidence: pass needs the pooled gate, OOS/IS >= 0.5, >= 60% of non-thin folds profitable, no single fold supplying all profit, profit factor >= 1.3 and expectancy >= 0.1R after costs. Labels: Low evidence < 30 trades, Provisional 30-99, Trusted >= 100 over at least 2 regimes.
- Review round 1 (PR 83): every config value is also a floor (a looser value fails to load, a tighter one loads; floors in `config.rs` `mod floor`, the first three compared with `risk.toml [gate.evidence]` by a parsing test). `policy_date` replaces `verified_on` (policy date, not a source check). `latest_for_all` is refused in the file (per-run opt-in). `Leg` gained `LegInstrument` (cash, future, option call/put) and `Product::Carry`. `EvidenceLabel::Exploratory` for approximate-cost runs. `Trials` methods take the in-run count and add `prior_trials` (saturating); callers must not add it. Walk-forward: a position open at the in-sample end is force-closed or discarded, never carried into OOS (slice 6 tests entry and exit timestamps). Provenance gained universe, parameter_sets, trial_penalty_pf; its date fields are required in a finished result. The pooled gate replaces the plan's earlier "per fold" text for AC 7.
- Config loader lives in `crates/backtest` (own typed errors); the floors (30 trades, PF 1.3, 0.1R) are checked against `risk.toml [gate.evidence]` by a test, so tightening is allowed and loosening fails to load.
- `LegSide` is a serde enum local to `backtest`; it is converted from `risk::types::Side` where the gate is called (slice 4). Types hold `Price`/`Qty` from core and `Decimal` rupees (serialised as strings).
- New dependencies for `backtest`: none outside the workspace set (`bazaar-core`, `chrono`, `chrono-tz`, `rust_decimal`, `serde`, `thiserror`, `toml`).

## Known limits
Calculator estimate, not a contract note; no delivery minimum; per-unit futures/options samples; history before 2026-11-20 has no verified rate set; survivorship and corporate-action adjustment UNVERIFIED unless the data source says so.
No as-of instrument lookup (TS-F9-2): `RunSpec` takes one `Instrument` for the whole run, so lot and tick history is not applied. Snapshots are stored (`instrument_snapshots`) but nothing reads them, and there is no typed error for a date without one. ## What was built
Slice 0: `types.rs` (`Leg`, `ChargeBreakdown`, `Fill`, `Trade`, `EquityPoint`, `Provenance`, `HistoryPolicy`, `EvidenceLabel`, `BacktestError`), `config.rs` (`BacktestConfig::load`, validation with `BacktestConfigError`, trial penalty helper), `config/backtest.toml`.
Slice 1 (cost model): `cost/mod.rs` `CostModel::new(cfg, policy, exchange)`; `charges_with_dp(&mut DpLedger, &Leg)` is the engine entry (DP once per scrip per IST day on the first delivery sell); `charges_excl_dp` omits DP and is for tests/line checks only. Rounding: STT and stamp whole rupees, others 2 dp, half-up after the full multiplication; GST on brokerage + txn + SEBI only; IPFT folded into `exchange_txn`. Strict policy gives `NoRateSet`; `latest_for_all` takes the newest set and `leg_is_approximate` counts off-date legs. Zero quantity or price, a basis mismatch (options need `premium`) and a missing row are `Invalid`. Matches the 5 owner calculator samples within Rs 0.05 per line (fixture `fixtures/charges/calculator_2026-11-20.json`).

Slice 4a (engine, `crates/backtest/src/engine.rs`): one instrument and one real `Strategy` per run; the loop decides on `DataView::ending_at(bars, i)` and fills at bar `i+1`; regimes come from a caller `RegimeProvider` (returns the whole `StrategyContext`, as of the closed bar only); sizing is `risk::size_with_cost` priced by the real cost model (entry and stop-exit legs plus a slippage estimate, never below `min_charges_slippage_buffer`; DP not in the estimate); every fill goes through `Ledger::apply`; intraday squares off at the entry bar close; open positions close at the last close (`TimeExit`); `slippage_multiplier < 1` is refused (QUAN-84-8). `EntryPolicy` has no non-test implementation: the DecisionGate adapter is 4c, lookahead property tests are 4b. Not modelled: pyramiding, partial exits, option-exercise STT, multi-instrument runs. Fix round (PR 88): intraday needs daily bars (sub-daily is refused); only NSE/BSE cash equity is accepted; a pending signal past `valid_until` is skipped; `NoRateSet`/`Overflow` from the cost model propagate (only real sizing refusals count as `skipped`); the engine calls `check_closed_bar` on every provider context; the time stop runs without a regime; `TightenStop` is snapped to the tick; DP is excluded from the sizing estimate (about Rs 15 per trade understated); single instrument: the whole capital is available to it, so pooled runs overstate capacity. Code is split into `engine/state.rs` and `engine/sizing.rs`. The DecisionGate adapter (mandatory before any result is shown) came in 4c. New edges: backtest depends on `bazaar-strategies` and `bazaar-risk`, and on `bazaar-regime` as a dev-dependency only (tests build `RegimeState`); the architecture allow-list gained `bazaar-regime` for backtest.

Slice 4b (`crates/backtest/tests/lookahead_props.rs`, AC 4, closes QUAN-88-9): a probe strategy asserts its `DataView` ends at the closed bar; `lookahead_future_bars_do_not_change_the_past` replaces every bar after `k` and requires identical observations, trades and equity up to `k`; `lookahead_entries_fill_at_the_next_open_plus_slippage` runs a strategy that peeks at the next close and still requires entry at the next open plus adverse slippage after a recorded signal on the previous bar; a regime provider that peeks is rejected; TS-88-1 (stop and target in one bar, the stop fills), plus a later-bar stop/target tie, a rising-series test proving entries occur, and a regression for the flat series with one high. No production change.

Slice 4c (`crates/backtest/src/gate_policy.rs`, the mandatory real-gate adapter): `GatePolicy` implements `EntryPolicy` over the real `bazaar_risk::Gate` and `config/risk.toml`; no approve-all exists outside tests and there is no override path (`Gate::decide` gets `None` and a sink that refuses every write). The gate clock is frozen at the decision bar's `close_time`; the snapshot (entries today, loss streak, day and week P&L, last losing exit per symbol, equity) is rebuilt from the closed trades, with any overflow or unknown input failing closed. The gate's `CostModel` is the engine's own round-trip estimate (`round_trip_cost`, extracted from sizing, so the gate re-sizes with the numbers the engine used) and is `Unknown` for sells. Every refusal is kept in `GatePolicy::rejections()` (rule ids, observed, limit, reason) and counted as `skipped`. Caller supplies what bars cannot give: evidence (G01), sector (G05) and an events closure. Engine change (4a code): `EntryPolicy::observe(&[Trade])` (default no-op), called at each decision bar; `fill_params` and `round_trip_cost` now `pub(crate)`. 12 unit tests incl. a clean entry through the real gate, unknown and weak evidence, an event day, a no-chase close, and a loss then a re-entry refused by G17.

Slice 2 (fill model, `crates/backtest/src/fill`): next-open entry with optional no-chase zone limit, gap-aware stop (`min`/`max` with open, then stop slippage), target fill only when traded through by `target_through_ticks`, stop wins a same-bar tie, time exit, adverse tick rounding of slippage and of stop/target levels, stop/target side validation, slippage multiplier (2x stress hook), lot floor. Target fills use entry-class slippage with no gap credit: a conservative choice per policy section 1 (decided, keep). Left out of this slice: AC4 peeking-double and `lookahead_` property tests (done in 4b), limit-entry fills, the no-`Fill`-with-default-charges test (QUAN-84-6), wiring `stress_slippage_multiplier` into the stress re-run (QUAN-84-3 hook exists as `FillParams::slippage_multiplier`).
Slice 3 (ledger): `ledger.rs` `Ledger::new(CostModel, capital)` owns one `DpLedger` (never share across runs); `apply(strategy_id, Fill, stop)` prices every fill via `charges_with_dp`, opens or fully closes a position (no pyramiding or partial exits; errors leave state untouched) and returns the `Trade` (gross after slippage, net after all charges, R to 4 dp, `None` when risk is 0); `square_off_intraday(date, exit_for)`; `mark` records net equity (capital + realised - open entry charges + unrealised); `max_drawdown` gives the largest rupee and percent fall.

Slice 3 fix round (PR 86 review): conservative conventions the engine must respect.
- Drawdown peak is seeded with starting capital (`max_drawdown(starting_equity, curve)`).
- Open positions are valued at gross unrealised minus entry charges; exit charges are NOT reserved (QUAN-86-5), so an open ledger is slightly optimistic. The engine must force-close at run end or report net of estimated exit charges.
- Marks carry forward per token over a missing bar; `mark` rejects time going backwards.
- Trades with no stop have `r_multiple = None` (QUAN-86-6): metrics must exclude them from R statistics and count them separately; the engine should reject stop-less entries where a stop is required.
- Opening validation: stop must be on the loss side; delivery is long only; an exit may not precede its entry; all arithmetic is checked.
- `square_off_intraday` stops at the first error; positions closed before it stay closed (CR-86-5). The slice exceeds the 400-line cap (CR-86-6), flagged only.
- New dev-dependency for the crate: `proptest` (workspace dep).

Slice 5 (metrics, provenance): `metrics.rs` `Metrics::from_trades` (all Decimal, no float: win rate, profit factor, expectancy R, net, drawdown from starting equity); trades with `r_multiple = None` are excluded from R stats and counted in `no_r_trades`. Profit factor with no losing trade is `None` and fails the bar (conservative). `assess` gives the label (Exploratory for approximate costs, Low below 30 pooled trades or `min_folds`, Trusted at 100 trades and 2 regimes unless the trial count caps it, else Provisional) and `meets_bar` (trade gate, folds, PF >= floor + trial penalty, expectancy); OOS/IS ratio and profitable-fold vote need per-fold data and come with slice 7. `provenance.rs`: SHA-256 config hashes, charges `verified_on` range over the whole file, `build` fills `Provenance`. New dependency: `sha2` (workspace dep, already used by bazaar-data). Fix round: PF and expectancy round toward zero (never across the bar); label and `meets_pooled_bar` (renamed, partial bar) count only trades with an R multiple (`no_r_trades` exposed); `charges_file_verified_range` covers the whole file (the used-sets range is `CostModel::used_verified_range`, slice 7b1); a run with no grid is one trial and trials are keyed by (strategy id, params).

Slice 6 (`crates/backtest/src/walkforward.rs`): rolling month-window folds after `warmup_bars` (OOS windows never overlap; partial windows dropped); per fold every candidate runs over the IS slice only, the best net P&L (ties: lowest index) is chosen and only it runs OOS; candidates are the trial count. A closure builds a fresh strategy, `RunSpec` and `GatePolicy` per window. G01 evidence for an OOS fold is `measurement_evidence` of the chosen candidate's IS metrics (window strictly before the first OOS bar, same costs and slippage); `walk_forward_pass = Known(true)` is set only in that constructor and is measurement context, not a verdict (the verdict comes from pooled OOS trades; never reuse for live or scanner gates). IS runs get no evidence and use a caller-supplied selection-only policy, never the OOS gate. A candidate missing the PF (floor plus trial penalty) or expectancy bar in IS gets no OOS run. Result: per-fold IS and OOS metrics, thin flags, pooled OOS trades and metrics, `assess` result, trials, and `gate_evidence` text for provenance. Limit (superseded by 7b3b): windows used to rebuild indicators from their own bars only; 7b3b supplies up to `warmup_bars` of past bars before each window as history-only warm-up, so a fold is thin for that reason only when the whole series before it is shorter than the strategy's warm-up.

Slice 7b3b (walk-forward regimes, warm-up, caller tests): `Trade.regime` is the market trend (`Up`, `Down`, `Range`; `Mixed` and `Unknown` give `None`) of the bar the entry signal was decided on, set by the engine (`Ledger::tag_open`). `WfParams.regimes` is gone: `walkforward::regimes_covered(pooled OOS trades)` counts the distinct tags from the trades, not from a caller-supplied number; the tags are only as trustworthy as the closure that ran the engine (as documented on `Trade.regime`). History-only warm-up: `RunSpec.warmup` (first N bars are seen by strategies but nothing is decided, traded or marked on them; more than `bars.len()` is refused) and the walk-forward closure now receives `&WindowBars` (derefs to the window; `with_history()` and `warmup()` feed `RunSpec`), with up to `warmup_bars` past bars before each IS and OOS window (OOS history includes IS bars, which are past data). The first decision of a window is on its first bar's close. Caller-side tests with the real engine: OOS bars rewritten leaves fold 0's IS trades and metrics identical; the OOS `GateEnv.evidence` equals `measurement_evidence` of the chosen IS metrics; QUAN-94-8 closed for the walk-forward caller: the closure builds its `RunSpec` from the runner's `CostModel` and the returned `ChargesEvidence` equals `from_legs` over ALL entry and exit legs of every window. `bazaar-regime` moved from dev-dependency to dependency of backtest (already allow-listed). Fix round (PR 99): a regime counts only with `evidence.min_trades_per_regime` pooled OOS trades (config key, floor 10; the owner approved 10 on 2026-11-21), so one trade is not a regime; the tag strings are explicit (`up`, `down`, `range`) and pinned by tests; `Ledger::tag_open` errors when no position is open; `walk_forward_gated` recomputes every trade leg's charges (all but the stateful DP) under the runner's `CostModel` and returns `Invalid` on a mismatch, so a closure built from another cost model is refused (the stateful DP charge is not rechecked, so a different `[dp]` table is not caught); the tags themselves are only as trustworthy as the closure (documented on `Trade.regime`); the look-ahead property test has a warm-up axis and close-based trend tags, and the real-engine caller test uses dated rate sets so each class of leg is proven to be in the evidence range.
