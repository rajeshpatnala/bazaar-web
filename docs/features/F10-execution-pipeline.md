## Paper account (slice 4, `paper::account`)
Cash is derived by replaying the current epoch's append-only ledger (kinds opening, buy, sell, charge); a fill books its charge and trade lines in one all-or-nothing `PaperLedgerRepo::append_many`. Charges come only from `ChargeSource` (zero or negative is refused). The funds check refuses a fill that would take cash below zero; a sell above the held quantity (passed in by the caller from the position book) is refused. `reset` uses only the configured `paper_starting_cash_inr`. One `PaperAccount` per ledger (single writer).

## Slice 5 (paper fills, `paper::{fill,executor,squareoff}`)
`FillSimulator` is pure and seeded (splitmix64; the seed only scales partial sizes to 50-100% of the participation cap). Market orders fill on the first tick strictly after acceptance, slipped against the trader and tick-aligned, clamped inside the circuits; limit orders fill at the limit only when the tick trades strictly through; no fill on stale or future ticks (order flagged), outside the normal session (decided inside the executor from the injected `MarketCalendar`, never by the caller; calendar error = closed), or for a buy at the upper / sell at the lower circuit. `PaperExecutor::place` validates (lot, tick, freeze, position net of working sells, funds estimate) and never fills; fills book through `PaperAccount::apply_fill`; a square-off fill is audited write-ahead (audit failure undoes the fill, it retries on the next tick). `SquareOff::on_clock` cancels working MIS orders and queues opposite market orders once per IST date. The square-off time is a `ValidatedSquareOffTime`, only buildable against the calendar for every segment in use (RISK-119-3); a carried MIS position is queued at the first clock call of a new date (RISK-119-10); a failed queue (missing spec, overflow) is reported and retried. Working buys reserve funds at placement (RISK-119-8). Cancellations, DAY expiries, square-off fills and square-off failures are audited (RISK-119-5); a square-off is never dropped on a ledger or charge error, it retries. `accept` is crate-private; `place(&ApprovedOrder)` is the only public entry (RISK-119-1).

RISK-119-6 decision: paper keeps the audit write-ahead and fails closed (no reduce-only bypass). A square-off blocked by an audit outage appears in `TickOutcome::blocked_square_offs`, is logged at error each tick, and fills on the first tick after the audit recovers; the wiring must alarm on any entry.
Limits: MIS/NRML use full cash (no margin, no leverage), intraday short selling is refused, AMO is not modelled, DAY expiry is by date change only. Tests cannot mint `ApprovedOrder` outside `execution`, so `place` itself is covered through `accept`. `execution::types` now re-exports `Side` (paper may not depend on `risk`).

## 8b slice 3 (restart reload)
`Pipeline::reload()` (call once before `spawn`): (1) `ApprovalRepo::reconcile_stray_decisions` (new port method; SQLite wraps `approvals_reconcile`) expires, audited, any pending order that already has a decision row, so it is never re-presented (stricter than waiting for the TTL); an error aborts the reload. (2) `pending_approval` orders are re-queued with `queued_at` = the intent's `created_at` (the store keeps no queue time, so this is the conservative, earlier time); past-TTL and undecodable rows are expired, audited. (3) `approved`-but-never-sent orders, FAIL-CLOSED choice for risk-guardian: re-gated with a fresh quote, TTL-checked (before and after the quote) and guard-checked, and resumed ONLY if all pass; otherwise they end `blocked` (event `guard_blocked`, audited), nothing sent. The state machine has no Approved -> Expired edge, so `blocked` is the terminal. (4) `sent`/`partially_filled`/`unknown` orders are never touched or re-sent, only counted and logged. More than 1000 rows in a state is an error (nothing is sent). The submit payload now stores the full intent (`OrderIntent::to_payload`/`from_payload`: symbol, side, qty, kind and price as strings, product, reduce_only, created_at). Hard conditions 1 and 2 above are met by this slice. Tests: `reload_*` and `submit_stores_the_full_intent_*` in `execution/src/pipeline.rs`, `payload_round_trips_*` in `types.rs`, `reconcile_stray_decisions_*` in `store/src/port_impls.rs`. TS-132-2 is covered by `reload_resumes_an_approved_unsent_order_*` and `reload_requeued_intent_is_approved_with_a_fresh_regate`.

Fix round (PR 133): `reload` is idempotent (ids already queued or finished are skipped); `from_payload(id, payload, now)` rejects qty 0, empty or over-500-byte symbol, limit price <= 0 and `created_at` after now (a future time would stretch the TTL); restart-time expiries and blocks carry actor "system" and a `reason` ("restart_reload: ...") in the audit payload; a failed list of sent/partial/unknown orders is `ExecError::Storage`, never all-clear; a failed `approved` resolution is counted in `ReloadReport.errors`, not `blocked`.

**HARD CONDITIONS for the wiring slice (RISK-133-3, SEC-133-3, RISK-133-4, SEC-133-2, SEC-133-4):**
- Startup calls `reload()` exactly once, before `spawn`, and ABORTS on `Err` (no serving, no decide route); a failed reload can leave the queue partly loaded (it is cleared, but nothing may be served on the error). A wiring test must prove it.
- sent/partially_filled/unknown orders are only counted. A reconciler for these states MUST exist before `live-trading` can be enabled; the feature stays off until it does (pre-live checklist item).

## BZ-F10-S10 slice 0a, part 1 (cost pools, `paper::cost_pool`; unused until slice 4)
- `PaperSessionId` (P#n, never 0). `Pool`: quantity, share cost without charges, unsold buy charges; no stored average. A partial sale takes `ceil2` shares of cost and charges (each capped at what is left; the only rounding); a full exit takes the rest without dividing. Sale result = proceeds - sale charge - cost out - charge out. `open_loss(mark) = min(0, mark x qty - (cost + unsold charges))`. Errors are typed (`PoolError`), the pool unchanged. A zero buy cost or zero sale proceeds is refused (`ZeroAmount`; plan P15 pairing, buy < 0 < sell); a zero charge is allowed by the pool.
- Charges rule (P14, owner 2026-11-25): charges stay apart from the average and count with the sale, split by quantity; unsold buy charges count in the open loss. ## BZ-F10-S10 slice 0a, part 2 (ledger replay, `paper::cost_pool::replay`; unused until slice 4)
- `replay_pools(lines, today)` applies lines in the order given (ledger id order; timestamps never reorder them) and returns the pools (a sold-out pool stays, empty) and the sales, each dated by its sell line. Input is `ReplayLine`; its symbol, qty and product reach the stored ledger in slice 2.
- Pairing (P15): a charge pairs with the very next line, a buy or sell with the same session and `ref_id` (both present). Charge < 0, buy < 0 < sell, opening > 0. The first bad line or broken pairing fails the whole replay with `ReplayError { line, fault }`; a line is its 0-based position (an orphan charge reports the charge's).
- Also refused: unknown kind or product, session 0, empty symbol, qty 0 or missing, a timestamp that is not RFC 3339 at `+05:30` or is dated after today, a sale above what the pool holds.
- Bounds (SEC-185-2; code constants, sanity limits and not trading limits): at most 4 fraction digits (trailing zeros ignored), line amount at most 1e11, pool cost and unsold charges at most 1e12 each, pool quantity at most 1e8. Within them pool arithmetic is exact and a partial sale's paisa share is the true ceiling (property test at the bounds).
- TS-185-3: property over mixed buys and sales in 8 pools: sum of sale results = cash flow + cost held + unsold charges; a pool at quantity 0 is empty.

## BZ-F10-S10 slice 0b (session and shared figures, `paper::pnl`; unused until slice 4)
- `session_figures(lines, today, active, mark)` reads the whole ledger in id order and gives each session's day and week (since the IST Monday): sale results in the window, open losses of the pools held now, equity at the window start (opening plus earlier cash, plus share cost held then, unsold buy charges not added back). `shared_figures(sessions, starting_cash)` adds P&L over all sessions; base = smallest of starting cash and the equity of the active session and every session with lines in the window. Opening lines are never P&L.
- Refused (`PnlError`, never zero): every replay fault; a session whose first line is not its one opening line; a line dated before the one ahead of it in its session; no lines for the active session; shares held in another session; a missing mark (`mark` returns `None` when missing or stale; the age bound is the caller's, slice 4); a mark not positive, over 4 fraction digits or above 1e11 (`Pool::open_loss`); a sum above 1e15 (`MAX_SUM`, checked at every addition, across sessions too); a base of 0 or less.
